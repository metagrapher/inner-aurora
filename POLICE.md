# POLICE

## The Force

The police are the primary enforcers of law in the game. They find themselves in a strange position as a playable role, as they are both the enforcers of law and the lawbreakers. It would seem that there's nothing to check them, except themselves.

The Force is not always a pleasant or fair place to be. The rules are arbitrary, and if you don't meet them, you can get kicked off The Force.

The Force is a lucrative place to be, really, because you are the law, you kind of make the law. But not everyone can be a Cop. There are only so many cops in a city, it just depends on the Needs of The Force.

The Force goes where The Force is needed. You'll find that travel is different for you as an Officer of The Law. You can't just travel anywhere--you're not necessarily welcome in any city--and you'll find that when you go places, well, things are different.

### Undercover Cops

Most cops are undercover, really. Everyone wants to be undercover!
The cool thing about being undercover is that you get to interact like a normal player, but you can bust the NIMBYs and the Entrepreneurs when they do bad things. (actually anyone can "bust" people, but if you're not a cop, then you're just a vigilante and you can get busted yourself.) As a uniformed officer on the beat, however, you're less likley to find crime out in the open. It might depend on where you are...

And you can't be undercover forever. You have to work the beat sometimes. Look, the job's not always glamorous, okay!
