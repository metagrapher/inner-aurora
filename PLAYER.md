# PLAYERS 

## STATS
Players have stats like:

Health
Strength
Hunger
Stamina
Birthday

### Birthday
You are born 18 game years ago. You get a birthday as soon as you are created, welcome to life!
Game time is 1 minute to 1 real second. So then, if you are 18 game years old, you are 18 x 365 x 24 x 60 real seconds old.

### Strength
Strength is just a number! okay, it literally is just a number, a number that you can build up, or which can atrophy over time if you don't work out. Good for you, it atrophies slowly. What's better is that for a time after working out, your strength increases every tick during your recovery period. After that period, your strength will atrophy just a little bit every tick.

### Stamina

The way that stamina works is that it depletes throughout the day, and if you don't do something to keep yourself awake, you're going to fall asleep.

You have to eat to keep your stamina up, and your health. If you are out of stamina, and you are not asleep, you begin to lose health.

### Health

Health is a stat that depletes over time, only if you are extremely tired, or poisoned. If you are out of health, you die. We are undecided about resurrections.

### Hunger

Hunger will crop up from time to time, every 4-8 hours while you are awake, depending on how much you eat. Hunger is a stat that increases slowly over time, usually in coordinate with your calories. As you do things you accrue caloric debt, or negative calories. Basically, you consume calories throughout the day. Activities consume calories (they accrue negative calories). And you have to gather calories by eating. So, you have a calorie budget. If that budget gets low, you are hungry. But hunger is a separate meter, because you can also deplete hunger by using drugs. So, as complicated as it is, hunger is a thing that can go up and down, and it's separate from calories.

### Calories

Eating food will increase calories. small meals are only 500 calories, while large meals are 1000 calories. 

Activities consume calories (they accrue negative calories).  So walking a mile costs 60 calories. Running a mile costs 300 calories, so running away costs calories, ~100x strength. Driving costs 15 calories per mile. Riding the bus/subway costs 20 calories per mile. Flying on a plane costs 20 calories per hour (game time), so does riding a train between cities.

## PROPERTY
Players can have property, like cars, houses, and things. Things take up space. They go in houses and cars, and sometimes pockets.

### HANDS / CARRYING THINGS
You can carry things in your hands, but everyone can see them. This increases your value as a target for robbery.

### POCKETS
All players have two front pockets and a back pocket. 
Your pockets hold a certain amount, consult BAGS&HOLDING.md for more specifics.

### LUGGAGE
You can carry a backpack without impeding your hands.
You can get other luggage (rolling luggage) that will increase your capacity to carry things, but will also occupy a hand.

### CAR
You can buy a car. 
Cars can hold things, sometimes luggage, perhaps?
Cars can be referenced in the CARS.md file.

### HOME
You probably need a place to live. You need to rent a home, or maybe buy one.
Why stop at one home? Own many. Rent homes in different districts so that you can stay overnight there without having to travel around, maybe, or maybe you need a stash house to hold things. Houses can hold things.

Maybe you don't have a home right now, but you have a car. Maybe your home is a tent. Maybe you don't have a home at all.

## CURFEW

At 23:00 every night, if you don't have a home in that city, you are sleeping rough. The fact is, your stamina depletes throughout the day, and if you don't do something to keep yourself awake, you're going to fall asleep outside.
