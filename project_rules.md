
## Testing & Environment
- **Testing URL**: ALWAYS use `http://localhost:6969` for browser-based testing, previews, and verification.
    - DO NOT use ports 3000, 4321, or 8787 unless explicitly told otherwise.
    - This project uses a custom port (`6969`) defined in `package.json` and `astro.config.mjs`.


## Communication
- **Terminlogy**: DEFINITELY AND ABSOLUTELY AVOID the term "user". 
    - Do not refer to the developer as "user". Use "Developer", "Architect", or "You".
    - Do not refer to "the user" as a generic entity.
    - There is no "the user".
