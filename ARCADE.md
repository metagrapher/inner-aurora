# The Arcade

There's an arcade in every city. It's actually a portal to save your game and to return to arca.de.com to play other games!

### Other games and ideas

#### BADONKADONKY KONG

treat html pages like parts of donkey kong. avoid falling emoji that roll down at you after the html structure gets wrecked

#### LINE SKATER

a line rider game that lives on top of other HTML pages allowing you to tony hawk your little stick figure around the html page you are on.  Mash buttons and do sick tricks and rack up points.

#### SNAKE 

Classic snake, nokia style.
